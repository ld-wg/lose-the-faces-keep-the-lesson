\begin{abstract}

% Factual draft (2026-10-22), revised 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30: translation of the resumo.
Classroom recordings expose students' and teachers' identities. This work
investigates whether swapping every detected face for a synthetic identity
reduces automatic face re-identification while keeping behavioral signal
(expression, pose, emotion), measured on the video a viewer sees. The pipeline is
hybrid and has three stages: SCRFD-10GF detection with tracking and a recall
post-pass; one Stable Diffusion XL synthetic identity per track, swapped onto the
face in every frame by \texttt{inswapper} with GFPGAN restoration; and fail-closed
composition, which hides every detected face that has no valid replacement. The
main contribution pushes the swap's identity embedding away from the track's
estimated real identity. On three short videos (29\,s in total), at the chosen
operating point, held-out FaceNet rank-1 drops from
\resulten{e3c/pooled/native/facenet.rank1} to \resulten{e3c/pooled/b-1.2/facenet.rank1},
with no measurable expression or pose cost and a non-significant drop in emotion
agreement (\resulten{e3c/pooled/native/emotion_kappa} to
\resulten{e3c/pooled/b-1.2/emotion_kappa}). Among the faces that receive a
synthetic face, the final pipeline is re-identified at
\resulten{derived/frontier/e5-ref/rank1} while covering
\resulten{derived/frontier/e5-ref/share} of the faces, against
\resulten{derived/frontier/e2-blanket/rank1} for the released BLANKET, which covers
\resulten{derived/frontier/e2-blanket/share}; on the final video, however, methods
that hide more faces score a lower rank-1, and blur, mosaic and GANs score below the
pipeline. Gradient guidance inside the diffusion sampler and identity-code
optimization in a generative adversarial network have no measurable effect.
Expression error stays at \resulten{e3e/pooled/gate/expression} inter-ocular
units, about four times its noise floor, and no method keeps emotion. The projected
per-face loop cost is \resulten{e5/pooled/adapt-0.08/class-hours} GPU hours per
hour of class. The result is partial face de-identification, another point on the
privacy--coverage--utility trade-off, not anonymization: hair, head outline,
clothing and voice remain.

\end{abstract}
